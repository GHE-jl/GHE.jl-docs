References

The methods implemented in this package are drawn from the following sources.

Convolution methods

  • Marcotte, D., & Pasquier, P. (2008). Fast fluid and ground temperature computation for geothermal ground-loop heat exchanger systems. Geothermics, 37(6), 651–665. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.geothermics.2008.08.003 The FFT-based convolution behind convolution.

  • Pasquier, P., & Marcotte, D. (2013). Efficient computation of heat flux signals to ensure the reproduction of prescribed temperatures at several interacting heat sources. Applied Thermal Engineering, 59(1–2), 515–526. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2013.06.018

  • Beaudry, G., Pasquier, P., & Nguyen, A. (2024). New formulations and experimental validation of non-stationary convolutions for the fast simulation of time-variant flowrates in ground heat exchangers. Science and Technology for the Built Environment, 30(3), 208–219. https://doi.org/10.1080/23744731.2023.2279468 The non-stationary convolution behind convolution_ns.

Fluid temperature and g-functions

  • Claesson, J., & Javed, S. (2011). An analytical method to calculate borehole fluid temperatures for time-scales from minutes to decades. ASHRAE Transactions, 117(PART 2), 279–288. The mean-fluid-temperature framework underlying fluid_temperature.

  • Pasquier, P., Zarrella, A., & Labib, R. (2018). Application of artificial neural networks to near-instant construction of short-term g-functions. Applied Thermal Engineering, 143, 910–921. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2018.07.137 The PublishedANN model behind short_term_response and short_term_nodes.

  • Pasquier, P., & Marcotte, D. (2020). Robust identification of volumetric heat capacity and analysis of thermal response tests by Bayesian inference with correlated residuals. Applied Energy, 261, 114394. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2019.114394 The DeepANN model (wider validity ranges, the default) behind short_term_response and short_term_nodes.

  • Dusseault, B., Pasquier, P., & Marcotte, D. (2018). A block matrix formulation for efficient g-function construction. Renewable Energy, 121, 249–260. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.renene.2017.12.092

  • Nguyen, A., & Pasquier, P. (2021). A successive flux estimation method for rapid g-function construction of small to large-scale ground heat exchanger. Renewable Energy, 165, 359–368. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.renene.2020.10.074

Load profile and resistance

  • Bernier, M. A., Pinel, P., Labib, R., & Paillot, R. (2004). A Multiple Load Aggregation Algorithm for Annual Hourly Simulations of GCHP Systems. HVAC&R Research, 10(4), 471–487. https://doi.org/10.1080/10789669.2004.10391115 The synthetic annual load profile generated by ground_load_profile.

  • Javed, S., & Spitler, J. (2017). Accuracy of borehole thermal resistance calculation methods for grouted single U-tube ground heat exchangers. Applied Energy, 187, 790–806. The effective borehole resistance $R_b^*$ consumed from BoreholeResistance.jl.
